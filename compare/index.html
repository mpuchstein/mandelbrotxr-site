Compare two candidates

Pick any two of the 8 candidates to see them side by side: the rank every judge gave them, their phase-1 review scores, the judges' verbatim justifications, the special elements the judges noted for each (strengths and problems), and screenshots. The selection lives in the URL.

Choose A and B, then press Compare (or Enter). Every pair has its own address, compare/<a>-vs-<b>/, so a comparison can be bookmarked and shared.

Read with care: Two prompts · No headset · Same model family
  • Two prompts. v0 and v1 are different prompts (v0 asks for three.js and three.xr.js; v1 asks for WebXR and the "highest possible visual wow effect"). Each candidate was judged against its own prompt, in one combined ranking.
  • No headset. No real VR or AR session was entered. Everything was tested in desktop Chrome, without an XR device or emulator; phone widths were simulated.
  • Same model family. All reviewers and judges were Opus 5.5 agents, and two of the eight candidates are Opus 5.5 builds.
