B GPT6 Pro · v0

  • Done-when fulfilment 8 — Immersive VR, full-size AR and tabletop AR rooms are available through native WebXR. iPhones get a 3D touch, motion and stereo room, which is the best possible without WebXR on iOS.
  • Device/XR compatibility 8 — Correct feature detection, local-floor/hand-tracking/dom-overlay requested as optional, four input sources (controllers, hands, transient-pointer, screen taps) and a secure-context check. There is no hit-test and no hand meshes.
