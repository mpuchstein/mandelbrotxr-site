The reviewers' one-line reasons, verbatim

A GPT6 Pro · v0

  • Done-when fulfilment 8 — Immersive VR, full-size AR and tabletop AR rooms are available through native WebXR. iPhones get a 3D touch, motion and stereo room, which is the best possible without WebXR on iOS.
  • Device/XR compatibility 8 — Correct feature detection, local-floor/hand-tracking/dom-overlay requested as optional, four input sources (controllers, hands, transient-pointer, screen taps) and a secure-context check. There is no hit-test and no hand meshes.
  • Visual wow effect 6 — A well-composed gallery with signage, a live minimap wall and a glass case. The fractal itself is a coarse vertex-coloured heightfield (at most 289×217 vertices) with smoothed cliff normals and no env map.
  • Usability & controls 8 — Excellent 2D UI, guide modal, minimap and presets, plus an in-world 9-button panel, teleport rings and snap turn. The VR panel is low (centre at 0.65 m), there is no hover feedback, and the AR tabletop panel sits in front of the exhibit.
  • Code quality & robustness 7 — Robust: worker with fallback, generation IDs, transferables, context-loss handling and user activation preserved for requestSession. However, the app code ships minified (single-letter identifiers), and palette changes trigger full recomputes.
  • Prompt adherence 8 — Truly standalone (three r186 embedded, zero network dependencies). three.xr.js is replaced by WebXRManager, with the reason explained in the in-page guide (three.xr.js is archived and uses obsolete APIs).

B Copilot Interactive · v1

  • Done-when fulfilment 2 — A VR/AR session starts, but the XR rendering is broken: each eye sees a different half of the image, the camera faces backwards, pitch is inverted, and there is no actual room.
  • Device/XR compatibility 3 — Requests immersive-vr/ar with local-floor, but ignores view.projectionMatrix, has no controller/hand input, no local fallback, and iPhones get only an inline orbit view.
  • Visual wow effect 4 — The neon raymarched glow slab could look nice on a flat screen, but it marches a non-distance field, colours by integer iteration counts (banding), the "stars" are stripes, and there is no room.
  • Usability & controls 2 — Desktop and touch orbit/pinch work. In XR there is no input, no exit UI and no fractal navigation, and the hints promise features that don't exist ("tap to place", ESC).
  • Code quality & robustness 3 — Compact, syntax-valid raw WebGL, but the XR camera math is wrong, the shader costs about 8k Mandelbrot iterations per pixel, and a failed session start leaves the session hanging.
  • Prompt adherence 6 — A true standalone single file with no dependencies that uses WebXR, but "virtual room" and "highest wow / good usability" are not delivered.

The reviewers' one-line reasons, verbatim

A GPT6 Pro · v0

  • Loads & renders 9 — Load event at ~136 ms, 3D room and relief visible at first screenshot; zero console messages across every session.
  • Done-when (desktop-checkable) 8 — A real gallery room around a 3D Mandelbrot relief on a plinth; the XR path is present but correctly disabled here. It deliberately does not use three.xr.js, and says why.
  • Visual wow 8 — Polished "observatory" look: plinth, glass case, wall plaques, halo ring, three palettes. Zoomed presets look weaker (thin floating slab).
  • Usability & controls 8 — Orbit, scroll, walk mode with WASD and an on-screen pad, minimap click-to-center, presets, palettes, detail and relief controls, plus a thorough guide. On narrow screens the Explore panel hides the view, and the header icons are 34 px.
  • Robustness 8 — No errors. Unavailable fullscreen and motion sensors are handled with clear toasts, and stereo exits cleanly. Frame rate could not be measured in this environment (see notes).
  • XR readiness & messaging 8 — isSessionSupported gating; "VR unavailable here" is a disabled button with a plain explanation. AR (room and tabletop) is hidden when unsupported. Split-screen stereo and a gyro fallback are offered. Untestable without a device.

B Copilot Interactive · v1

  • Loads & renders 7 — Loads instantly with no console errors; a styled landing page and an animated WebGL backdrop appear within about 1 s.
  • Done-when (desktop-checkable) 3 — The 3D scene is mostly glowing wavy "ribbon" walls. The Mandelbrot set shows up only as a faint, sparse purple speckle cloud that is barely visible.
  • Visual wow effect 4 — The neon-tube aesthetic and typography look good, but the fractal, which is the subject, is nearly invisible: no colour bands, no glow, no recognisable cardioid.
  • Usability & controls 4 — There is one button. Drag-orbit works, wheel zoom has no visible effect, and there are no keys, presets or palettes. The overlay can't be hidden and covers the left third of the scene.
  • Robustness 6 — There are no JS errors and the phone layout reflows cleanly. rAF ran at about 2 fps here, which is probably partly the test environment (inconclusive).
  • XR readiness & messaging 4 — The button is labelled "Launch 3D room", not "Enter VR". On unsupported devices it still calls requestSession and fails. The fallback message points to a "3D touch view" that doesn't exist as a separate control.
