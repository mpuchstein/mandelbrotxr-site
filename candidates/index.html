Candidates

8 standalone WebXR Mandelbrot pages, each built by an AI coding assistant from one of the task prompts. They are listed by mean rank across the 5 blind judges. Every card links to a page with the product, its reviews, every judge's verbatim justification, screenshots and facts.

Mean rank derived (on every card below) is the arithmetic mean of a candidate's ranks over the judges that ranked it. Standing = 1 + the number of candidates with a strictly lower mean rank, among those ranked by every judge (a competition rank: ties share a place, e.g. 1, 1, 3; shown as “(tie)”). Phase-1 overall scores are the 1–10 scores the blind reviewers wrote, one per review type. Full rankings · caveats.
