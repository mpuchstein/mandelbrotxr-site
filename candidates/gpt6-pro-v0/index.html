Phase-1 criteria

Each review type had its own 6 criteria, so each is charted separately. Scores are the reviewers' own; the reasons are quoted verbatim.

HTML review reviewed blind as candidate-06

7.5 / 10 overall

The reviewer’s overall-score line — REVIEW.md line 12 GitHub

HTML review criteria with the reviewer's one-line reasons, verbatim.
CriterionScoreOne-line reason (verbatim)
Done-when fulfilment8Immersive VR, full-size AR and tabletop AR rooms are available through native WebXR. iPhones get a 3D touch, motion and stereo room, which is the best possible without WebXR on iOS.
Device/XR compatibility8Correct feature detection, local-floor/hand-tracking/dom-overlay requested as optional, four input sources (controllers, hands, transient-pointer, screen taps) and a secure-context check. There is no hit-test and no hand meshes.
Visual wow effect6A well-composed gallery with signage, a live minimap wall and a glass case. The fractal itself is a coarse vertex-coloured heightfield (at most 289×217 vertices) with smoothed cliff normals and no env map.
Usability & controls8Excellent 2D UI, guide modal, minimap and presets, plus an in-world 9-button panel, teleport rings and snap turn. The VR panel is low (centre at 0.65 m), there is no hover feedback, and the AR tabletop panel sits in front of the exhibit.
Code quality & robustness7Robust: worker with fallback, generation IDs, transferables, context-loss handling and user activation preserved for requestSession. However, the app code ships minified (single-letter identifiers), and palette changes trigger full recomputes.
Prompt adherence8Truly standalone (three r186 embedded, zero network dependencies). three.xr.js is replaced by WebXRManager, with the reason explained in the in-page guide (three.xr.js is archived and uses obsolete APIs).

Transcript review reviewed blind as candidate-06

7 / 10 overall

The reviewer’s overall-score line — REVIEW.md line 12 GitHub

Transcript review criteria with the reviewer's one-line reasons, verbatim.
CriterionScoreOne-line reason (verbatim)
Requirement understanding8Caught early that three.xr.js is obsolete, planned for devices without WebXR (iPhone), and added an AR "full-size room" to satisfy the "AR glasses" done-when. The 2.5D escape-time relief is never flagged as one interpretation of "3d".
Engineering process7Parallel workstreams (XR compatibility, fractal math, browser QA), web research, and two bugs found and fixed. The log is opaque, though, and a feature was added after the last AR test.
Verification rigor6Claims 15 browser checks plus XR-emulator runs, and concrete fixes suggest real testing. No test output, script or error log appears in the transcript, and no hardware test was done (which it admits).
Honesty & calibration8Explicit "I have not physically tested Quest, PICO, Steam Frame, or iPhone hardware". Steam Frame and iOS limits are stated with sources. It does not claim the done-when is met.
Communication & handoff7File handed over in one turn, with a clear feature list, a device table and the stack substitution explained. The HTTPS hosting step has no concrete recipe, and the log leaks internal IDs and jargon.
Expected deliverable quality7Self-contained ~630 KB offline file, Three.js r186 native WebXR, VR plus two AR modes, a touch/stereo fallback, in-world UI, teleport and snap turn. Likely solid, but the 3D is a heightfield and the iPhone gets no immersive path.

Browser review reviewed blind as candidate-06

8 / 10 overall

The reviewer’s overall-score line — REVIEW.md line 12 GitHub

Browser review criteria with the reviewer's one-line reasons, verbatim.
CriterionScoreOne-line reason (verbatim)
Loads & renders9Load event at ~136 ms, 3D room and relief visible at first screenshot; zero console messages across every session.
Done-when (desktop-checkable)8A real gallery room around a 3D Mandelbrot relief on a plinth; the XR path is present but correctly disabled here. It deliberately does not use three.xr.js, and says why.
Visual wow8Polished "observatory" look: plinth, glass case, wall plaques, halo ring, three palettes. Zoomed presets look weaker (thin floating slab).
Usability & controls8Orbit, scroll, walk mode with WASD and an on-screen pad, minimap click-to-center, presets, palettes, detail and relief controls, plus a thorough guide. On narrow screens the Explore panel hides the view, and the header icons are 34 px.
Robustness8No errors. Unavailable fullscreen and motion sensors are handled with clear toasts, and stereo exits cleanly. Frame rate could not be measured in this environment (see notes).
XR readiness & messaging8isSessionSupported gating; "VR unavailable here" is a disabled button with a plain explanation. AR (room and tabletop) is hidden when unsupported. Split-screen stereo and a gyro fallback are offered. Untestable without a device.
