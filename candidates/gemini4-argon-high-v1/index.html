Warnings

Loads 3 external resources at runtime

A build-time scan of holographic_3d_mandelbrot_webxr_sanctuary.html found these absolute URLs loaded as scripts, stylesheets or other resources. The product needs a network connection to them, and its page needs a CSP that allows them (see the play-page notes in site/DEPLOY.md).

  • https://cdn.tailwindcss.com
  • https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js
  • https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@300;400;500;600;700&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@400;500;600;700&family=Orbitron:wght@500;700;900&display=swap

Open on a device

Scan to open the product on a phone or headset browser. The code encodes the product's absolute URL:

https://mandelbrotxr.puchstein.dev/play/gemini4-argon-high-v1/

WebXR sessions need HTTPS and a browser with WebXR support (for example the Meta Quest Browser or Chrome on Android). No headset session was entered during the experiment; see the caveats.
