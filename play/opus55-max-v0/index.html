Mandelbrot room

z ↦ z² + c, as a landscape you can walk around

Every point’s height shows how long its orbit resists escaping; the black plateau never escapes. Point at the landscape to zoom in, more than a trillion times.

How to use it
  • Headset: trigger or pinch on the landscape zooms in, grip zooms out, the panel next to the plinth has the rest.
  • Screen: drag to orbit, tap or click to zoom in, right-click zooms out.
  • Phone headset: put the phone in a Cardboard-style viewer in landscape; tapping the screen selects what the centre ring points at.
  • Keys: - zoom out, Backspace back, t tour, s shape, c colours, [ ] detail.
