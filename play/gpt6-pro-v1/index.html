YOUR GUIDE TO INFINITY

A room worth exploring.

This is a real, three-dimensional landscape of the Mandelbrot set. Walk around it, look over its ridges, or dive into its boundary.

Touch
Drag to orbit. Pinch to approach. Double-tap the landscape to zoom into that point. Use Walk in room for a movement pad.
Mouse
Drag to orbit, scroll to approach, double-click the landscape to zoom into it. Choose a destination to find a new detail.
Keyboard
WASD / arrows to walk; R to return home; H to hide the interface; Esc to close panels.
Headset
Point and press the trigger, or pinch, to use the in-room controls. Point at the floor and select to teleport. The right stick turns in 30° steps. Squeeze a grip to bring the panel to you. With no tracked input, look at a button for three seconds.

A two-dimensional set, expressed in three dimensions

zn+1 = zn² + c

Start with z₀ = 0 and repeat. For each point c in the complex plane, the calculation either escapes or stays bounded. Here, escape time becomes height and color. Dark raised regions have not escaped within the current iteration limit; they approximate the set. This is an escape-time relief, not a Mandelbulb.
